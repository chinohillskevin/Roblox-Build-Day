No blank-page panic

Start with a small idea. Make it playable.

You don’t need a giant story or a million features. Pick a simple loop that sounds fun, then build the first version.

Idea starters

What would you make?

Skyline Sprint

Race across rooftops, collect power-ups, and beat your best time.

Obby in a Box

Build a tiny obstacle course with one surprising rule.

Snack Shop

Serve silly customers before the timer runs out.

Deep Sea Dash

Explore, dodge hazards, and find the hidden treasure.

Hide & Seek: Space Station

Design a map with clever hiding spots and a clear way to win.

Your weird idea

Combine two things that should not go together. That’s often where the fun starts.

Creator prompt

Make it yours with three questions.

01

What do players do?

Run, build, trade, discover, protect, decorate, or solve?

02

What makes it tricky?

Add a timer, a twist, a rival, or a choice that changes the play.

03

What makes it yours?

Give the world a look, sound, or story only you would think of.

↗

Then press play.

Build the smallest version, test it, and let the next idea find you.
