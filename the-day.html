One day. Four moves.

Here’s how a build becomes a game.

You’ll move from “I have an idea” to “press play” with guided activities, friendly mentors, and lots of room to experiment.

The Build Day rhythm

Follow the path. Make it your own.

Build Day is broken into short, focused moments. Each one gives you a new tool, then time to use it in your own way. By the end, your project doesn’t have to be huge — it just needs to be yours and playable.

  • Short demos that make Studio feel less mysterious.
  • Hands-on time to build, test, and ask questions.
  • A low-pressure share-out to celebrate your progress.
Parent note

Build Day is beginner-friendly. A young builder can participate with a laptop and Roblox account, with an adult nearby for setup and support.
